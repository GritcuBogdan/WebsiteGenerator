Not every game in the lobby will count equally toward fulfilling the wagering requirement. Slots typically contribute the full amount, but table games, video poker, and live dealer games often contribute only a fraction of each bet placed. This can dramatically affect how quickly you are able to clear the bonus, so it is important to know which games you prefer and how they are weighted. The third factor is the time limit imposed on the promotion. Most welcome bonuses come with an expiration date, and if you do not complete the wagering requirement within that timeframe, your bonus funds and any associated winnings may be forfeited. These three elements—requirement size, game weighting, and time limit—are all clearly stated in the promotion's terms and conditions. Taking a few minutes to review these details before you opt in can make a significant difference in your overall experience and success rate with the offer.
