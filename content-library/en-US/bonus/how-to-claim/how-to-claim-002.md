Here is the process for claiming the {{WELCOME_BONUS}} at {{BRAND_NAME}}, broken down into its individual steps.

Steps to claim the welcome bonus:
- Register a new account and complete the email confirmation step
- Opt in to the welcome offer during signup, or enter the required promotional code if one applies
- Make a qualifying deposit that meets the minimum amount listed in the promotion terms
- Confirm the bonus funds have appeared in the account balance or promotions panel
- Read the wagering requirement, eligible games, and time limit before placing any bets

Skipping the opt in step is the most common reason a bonus fails to apply, so it is worth double-checking the promotions tab after registering if the offer does not appear automatically following a deposit. It is also worth remembering that the bonus amount received is directly tied to the size of the qualifying deposit when the offer is structured as a percentage match, so depositing less than the maximum simply results in a proportionally smaller bonus rather than a lost offer.
