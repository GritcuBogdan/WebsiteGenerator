Players new to {{BRAND_NAME}} sometimes assume the {{WELCOME_BONUS}} is applied automatically the moment an account is created, but in most cases claiming it requires a couple of deliberate actions. The process typically begins during registration, where an opt in option or promotional code field determines whether the offer is linked to the new account at all. Missing this step at signup is the single most common reason a bonus does not appear later.

After registering and confirming the account by email, the next required action is making a deposit that meets the minimum qualifying amount stated in the promotion. This deposit is what actually triggers the bonus credit, so an account that has opted in but not yet deposited will simply show the offer as pending rather than active. Once the deposit clears, the bonus funds should appear in the balance or the promotions panel within a short time, often instantly.

At that point, the important next step is reading the specific wagering requirement, game contribution rates, and expiry window attached to the offer, since these conditions govern how and when any winnings can eventually be withdrawn.
