Reading the fine print behind the {{WELCOME_BONUS}} at {{BRAND_NAME}} before opting in is one of the most useful habits a new player can develop. The advertised figure represents the maximum available under the offer, but the amount actually credited depends on the size of the qualifying deposit, since most welcome bonuses are structured as a percentage match up to that ceiling rather than a flat amount handed out regardless of deposit size.

Wagering requirements are the single most important term to understand, since they determine how many times the bonus amount, or in some cases the bonus plus the deposit combined, must be wagered before any winnings become eligible for withdrawal. Game contribution rates matter alongside this figure, because not every game counts equally toward meeting the requirement. Slots commonly contribute the full value of a wager, while table and live dealer games often contribute a smaller percentage or may be excluded entirely.

A time limit is typically attached as well, setting a deadline by which the wagering requirement must be completed. Letting that window close without finishing the requirement usually forfeits the remaining bonus balance, so tracking progress through the promotions page during the active period is worth doing regularly.
