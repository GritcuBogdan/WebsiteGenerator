Every {{WELCOME_BONUS}} at {{BRAND_NAME}} comes attached to a set of terms that determine how it actually functions in practice, and understanding them changes how the offer should be approached from the very first deposit. The most fundamental point is that bonus funds are not equivalent to cash the moment they are credited. They exist in a separate, restricted state until specific conditions are satisfied, most notably the wagering requirement, which sets out how many times the bonus amount must be played through before it converts into withdrawable funds.

Not all wagering counts equally. Game contribution rates vary, with slot games generally counting in full toward the requirement, while table games and live dealer formats often count for less, or occasionally not at all, since their odds and structure differ meaningfully from slot titles. There is also usually a maximum stake permitted per wager while bonus funds remain active, and exceeding it can void the offer entirely under some terms, so checking this limit before placing larger bets is worth doing.

Expiry is the final piece, setting a firm deadline for completing the wagering requirement. Once that period lapses, any unused or unconverted bonus balance is typically removed from the account automatically.
