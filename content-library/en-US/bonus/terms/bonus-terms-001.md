Before accepting the {{WELCOME_BONUS}} at {{BRAND_NAME}}, check the complete promotion panel and confirm that the offer matches the amount you intend to deposit. The headline value is normally the maximum available match. Your actual credit depends on the qualifying deposit, the activation rule, and any limit attached to the campaign. Bonus funds are not the same as cash, so they cannot normally be withdrawn immediately.

The most important conditions are the wagering multiplier, the game contribution rate, the time limit, and the maximum stake allowed while the offer is active. A slot may contribute the full value of a wager, while a table or live game may contribute a smaller percentage or may be excluded. The promotion page should show the current contribution table before you opt in.

| Condition | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | New account status, verified email, and one qualifying deposit |
| Activation | Whether you must opt in before depositing or playing |
| Wagering | The multiplier and whether it applies to bonus only or bonus plus deposit |
| Expiry | The number of days available to complete the requirement |
| Withdrawal | Any maximum conversion amount, stake cap, or verification step |

To claim the offer, create the account, complete the requested profile fields, review the terms, and make a qualifying deposit. Keep the promotion page open while you play so you can check progress and confirm which wagers count. If you decide that the conditions do not suit your budget, decline the offer before depositing. That keeps the balance simple and avoids a later request to remove active bonus funds.
