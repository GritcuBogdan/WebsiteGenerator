The {{FREE_SPINS_COUNT}} awarded at {{BRAND_NAME}} are typically restricted to a specific selection of slot games rather than usable across the entire catalogue. This is a standard practice across the industry, since spins are usually funded through an arrangement with a particular game or a small group of titles chosen for the promotion, rather than being a general credit that works everywhere. Most commonly, the eligible titles are video slots from the standard categories found in the main lobby, featuring familiar formats such as multiple paylines, bonus rounds, and a moderate stake range that suits promotional play.

Jackpot slots and progressive titles are less commonly included in free spins offers, since their prize pools and stake structures are usually managed separately, and the same is generally true for live dealer games and table games, which do not use a spin mechanic at all. Before using the awarded spins, checking the promotions panel to confirm exactly which title or titles they apply to is worth the extra moment, since attempting to use them on an ineligible game simply will not register any activity against the offer. Once you know the eligible game, spending a little time reviewing its rules and stake range helps you make the most of the spins provided.
