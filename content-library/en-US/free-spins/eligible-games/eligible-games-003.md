A common misconception about the {{FREE_SPINS_COUNT}} offered at {{BRAND_NAME}} is that they can be applied to any slot in the library. In practice, promotions of this kind are almost always tied to a specific title or a narrow category, chosen in advance as part of the campaign rather than left open to player choice. This is worth knowing before the spins are credited, so there is no surprise when opening the promotions panel to find that only one or two titles qualify.

The categories most commonly featured in these offers are standard video slots, generally titles with a familiar reel and payline structure and a stake range suited to promotional volumes of play. Jackpot slots are rarely included, largely because their funding structure works differently from a standard title, and games outside the slots category entirely, such as table games or live dealer formats, are never part of a free spins offer since the spin mechanic itself does not apply to them.

Before starting to play, take a moment to open the promotion details and confirm the exact eligible title or titles, along with the expiry window for any unused spins. This small check ensures the spins are put to use rather than left unclaimed once the offer period ends.
