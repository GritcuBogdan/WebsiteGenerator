Understanding which games the {{FREE_SPINS_COUNT}} at {{BRAND_NAME}} can be used on saves time and prevents confusion once the spins are credited.

Typical eligibility patterns for free spins offers:
- Standard video slots are the most common category included, particularly titles with a moderate stake range
- A single featured slot is often chosen for a specific campaign, meaning the spins may only work on that one title
- Jackpot and progressive slots are usually excluded, since their prize structures are handled outside standard promotions
- Table games and live dealer games are never eligible, since they do not use a spin mechanic
- Some broader campaigns allow spins across an entire slot category rather than a single title, though this is less common

Because eligibility can vary so much between campaigns, the safest approach is always to check the specific promotion details in the account dashboard before playing, rather than assuming a previous offer's rules still apply. Using the spins on the wrong game will simply not register, and depending on the platform, unused spins may still be lost once the stated time window closes, so confirming eligibility early gives the most value from the offer.
