Before settling on a slot to play at {{BRAND_NAME}}, it helps to understand what volatility actually measures and why it matters for the kind of session you can expect.

How volatility levels typically compare:
- Low volatility: frequent, smaller wins that produce a steadier balance over a longer session
- Medium volatility: a balance of moderate hit frequency and moderate payout size, often a good default choice
- High volatility: infrequent wins, but with the potential for significantly larger payouts relative to the stake

Volatility is separate from a game's return to player figure, which measures the average percentage returned to players over a very large number of spins. Two slots can share a similar return to player figure while feeling completely different to play, simply because one is built around frequent small wins and the other around rare large ones. Matching volatility to your own budget and preferred playing style, rather than chasing a specific payout size, tends to make for a more enjoyable and sustainable session regardless of which category you choose.
