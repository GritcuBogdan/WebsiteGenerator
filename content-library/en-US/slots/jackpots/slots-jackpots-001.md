Jackpot titles at {{BRAND_NAME}} add a visible prize ceiling to the ordinary slot format. Some jackpots are fixed, while others change as qualifying play contributes to a shared pool. The amount shown in the lobby should always be treated as a current display value rather than a promise that a particular title will pay during your session.

Before opening a jackpot title, read the contribution rule, the qualifying stake, and the payout conditions. Some formats require every payline or a specific bet level. Others can award a prize through a feature round that has its own rules. The information panel should also explain whether a win is paid in one balance update or subject to a staged withdrawal process.

| Jackpot detail | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Prize type | Shows whether the amount is fixed, progressive, or linked to a feature |
| Qualifying stake | Explains which bets can trigger the prize |
| Trigger rule | Identifies the combination or bonus round needed |
| Display timing | Clarifies how often the visible pool is refreshed |
| Payout rule | Explains settlement, limits, and any verification step |

Jackpot play should use the same budget discipline as any other slot. A larger headline prize does not change the probability of a result shown in the rules, and increasing a stake after a loss does not improve the odds. Compare the rules first, then decide whether the title fits the session you planned.
