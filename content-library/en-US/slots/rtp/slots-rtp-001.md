RTP and volatility describe different parts of a slot experience at {{BRAND_NAME}}. The return to player figure is a long term statistical measure based on a large number of spins. It is not a promise for one session and it does not predict the result of the next spin. Volatility describes how results may be distributed. A lower volatility title may produce smaller results more often, while a higher volatility title may create longer quiet periods and less frequent larger results.

These concepts help with comparison, but they do not remove chance. A title with a published RTP can produce a very different short term outcome because each spin is independent. Read the pay table, check the maximum stake rule, and choose a stake that leaves room for a complete session rather than increasing the amount after a losing run.

| Term | Practical meaning |
| --- | --- |
| RTP | A long term theoretical return used for comparison |
| Volatility | The expected pattern of smaller and larger results |
| Hit frequency | How often any winning combination may appear |
| Maximum win | The limit stated in the title rules or information panel |
| Session budget | The amount chosen before play and not exceeded during play |

RTP is most useful when read together with volatility, stake range, and feature rules. If the panel does not explain a figure clearly, ask support or choose another title with more transparent information. Good comparison starts with understanding the rules, not with assuming that a percentage guarantees a result.
