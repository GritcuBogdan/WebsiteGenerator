Many players assume eligibility is simply a matter of ticking a box during signup, but at {{BRAND_NAME}} it is treated as an ongoing condition of holding an account, not just a one-time confirmation. The age requirement is the clearest example. While the registration form only asks for a date of birth, that figure is cross-checked later against identification during verification, and an account found to belong to someone under the required age will be closed regardless of how long it has been active or what balance it holds.

The single-account rule works in a similar way. It is not enforced only by refusing a second signup with the same email, since duplicate accounts can also be identified through shared payment details, devices, or addresses. Players who are unsure whether a household member already holds an account, for instance, should raise the question with support before registering rather than risk an unintentional violation.

General eligibility also depends on accessing the service from a permitted location and providing information that is truthful and current. An address or contact detail that becomes outdated should be updated in the account settings, since accuracy here supports both communication and the verification process. Treating eligibility as something to maintain, rather than something to pass once, is the safest approach.
