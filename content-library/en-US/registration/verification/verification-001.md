After creating an account at {{BRAND_NAME}}, most players will be asked at some point to complete an identity verification process, often referred to as KYC, short for know your customer. This step is standard across regulated online casinos and exists to confirm that the account belongs to a real person who meets the age and eligibility requirements, and to help prevent fraud and money laundering. The two most commonly requested documents are proof of identity, such as a passport, national ID card, or driving license, and proof of address, such as a recent utility bill or bank statement showing your name and current address.

Verification is not always required immediately after signup. Many platforms allow a player to browse the site, and sometimes even make a modest deposit, before requesting documents. More commonly, the request is triggered when a player attempts their first withdrawal, since confirming identity before releasing funds protects both the player and the platform. Some accounts may also be flagged for verification earlier if the details provided during registration raise a question, such as a mismatch between the name on a payment method and the name on the account.

Uploading clear, unedited copies of the requested documents through the official account verification page is usually enough to complete the process within a day or two. Keeping these documents current and matching the details on file speeds up any future request.
