It is worth thinking of verification at {{BRAND_NAME}} less as a hurdle and more as the final step that unlocks full use of the account, particularly withdrawals. Proof of identity confirms who you are and that you meet the minimum age requirement, while proof of address confirms where you live, which matters for both legal compliance and fraud prevention. Together, these two document types form the backbone of most verification requests across the online casino industry.

The timing of the request can vary. Some players are asked to verify right after registering, especially if a detail on the form did not fully match automated checks. Far more commonly, verification is requested the first time a withdrawal is submitted, since this is the point at which confirming identity becomes most important. A smaller number of accounts may also be asked to reverify periodically or after a significant change, such as a new address or payment method.

Whatever the timing, the documents requested are almost always the same: a valid photo ID and a recent address document, occasionally paired with a payment method check. Keeping digital copies ready in advance, rather than searching for them once a withdrawal is already pending, is a simple habit that avoids unnecessary delay.
