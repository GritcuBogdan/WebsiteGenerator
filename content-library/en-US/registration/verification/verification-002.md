Identity verification at {{BRAND_NAME}} is a routine part of holding an account, and understanding what is typically requested makes the process much less stressful when it happens.

Documents commonly requested during verification:
- A government-issued photo ID, such as a passport, national identity card, or driving license
- Proof of current address, such as a utility bill, bank statement, or official government letter issued within the last few months
- A payment method confirmation, such as a photo of the card used for deposits with the middle digits partially hidden
- Occasionally, a selfie holding the ID document, used to confirm the person submitting matches the document owner

These requests are usually triggered by a first withdrawal request, since confirming identity before releasing funds is standard practice, though some accounts are asked to verify earlier if something about the registration details needs confirming. The review itself is typically handled by a dedicated compliance team, and turnaround time can range from a few hours to a couple of days depending on document quality and how busy the review queue is. Submitting clear, uncropped, and current documents the first time is the best way to avoid a second request and a longer wait before funds can be withdrawn.
