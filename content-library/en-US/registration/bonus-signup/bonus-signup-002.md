The {{WELCOME_BONUS}} at {{BRAND_NAME}} is designed to connect directly to the signup process, and there are a couple of common ways this works in practice.

How the welcome bonus typically connects to registration:
- An opt in checkbox appears somewhere in the registration form, letting new players choose to activate the offer during signup
- A promotional code field may be included instead, requiring a specific code to be entered before the account is created
- The offer may activate automatically once the account is confirmed, becoming ready as soon as a qualifying deposit is made
- Some platforms display the offer prominently on the registration page itself as an incentive to complete signup

Regardless of which method applies, the underlying idea is the same: the bonus is tied to the account from the moment of registration, but it is rarely credited immediately. Instead, it usually waits for a first qualifying deposit that meets the minimum amount specified in the promotion terms. Checking the promotions section of the account dashboard right after registering is a reliable way to confirm whether the offer was successfully linked, and to review the wagering and time limit conditions before making that first deposit.
