Many new players are drawn to {{BRAND_NAME}} specifically because of the {{WELCOME_BONUS}} on offer, and the good news is that claiming it usually starts right within the registration form itself. During signup, most platforms include a checkbox or toggle asking whether you would like to opt in to the current welcome promotion. Leaving this option selected, or actively choosing it if it is not selected by default, is what links the bonus to your new account before you have even made a deposit.

It is worth reading the short description attached to this opt in option, since it often summarizes the qualifying deposit amount and any basic conditions attached to the offer. Once the account is created and the email is confirmed, the bonus typically remains reserved for the account until the first qualifying deposit is made, at which point it is credited automatically. Some platforms instead require a promotional code to be entered during signup or at the cashier, so checking whether a code is required is worth doing before assuming the bonus will apply automatically.

If the opt in step is missed during registration, it is sometimes still possible to activate the bonus manually from the promotions tab in the account dashboard, though this varies by platform and by the specific terms of the offer, so checking soon after registering is the safest approach.
