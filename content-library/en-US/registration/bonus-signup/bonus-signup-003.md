A common question from new players is whether the {{WELCOME_BONUS}} at {{BRAND_NAME}} needs to be requested separately after registering, or whether it is part of the signup process itself. In most cases, it is the latter. The registration form is where the connection between a new account and the welcome offer is established, typically through a simple opt in toggle presented alongside the other signup fields.

This design exists for a practical reason. Presenting the bonus decision at signup, rather than afterward, ensures the player has agreed to the associated terms before any deposit is made, which keeps the process transparent. It also means that skipping past the opt in step without paying attention can result in registering an account without the bonus attached, so it is worth pausing on that specific field rather than clicking through the form quickly.

Once opted in during registration, the bonus generally sits inactive until the account is confirmed and a first deposit meeting the stated minimum is completed, at which point the bonus funds are added automatically. If the opt in was missed, checking the promotions tab shortly after registering is the next step, since some platforms allow manual activation there within a limited window after account creation.
