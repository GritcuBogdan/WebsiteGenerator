A cashout starts in the same place a deposit does. Open the cashier at
{{BRAND_NAME}}, choose from {{WITHDRAWAL_METHODS}}, enter an amount, and confirm
it. One thing to check first: if bonus funds are still in play, read the
promotion's terms before you submit, since withdrawing partway through can forfeit
an offer you have not finished.
