Understanding withdrawal limits at {{BRAND_NAME}} before requesting a payout helps set the right expectations for both the amount and the timing.

What to check before withdrawing:
- The minimum withdrawal amount for the specific method chosen among the {{WITHDRAWAL_METHODS}} available
- Any maximum amount allowed per single transaction
- Daily, weekly, or monthly withdrawal caps that may apply on top of the per-transaction limit
- The estimated processing time, both for internal review and for the payment provider itself
- Whether identity verification has already been completed, since this is usually a prerequisite for any withdrawal at all

Limits and processing times are not uniform across methods. E-wallets and similar digital options tend to have faster processing once approved, while bank transfers and card withdrawals typically take longer due to how those payment networks operate. Larger withdrawal requests that exceed a per-transaction cap may need to be split into multiple submissions, which can extend the total time before all funds arrive. Reviewing the cashier page for the current figures before submitting a request is the most reliable way to plan around them.
