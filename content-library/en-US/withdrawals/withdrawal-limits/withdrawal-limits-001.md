Withdrawal limits at {{BRAND_NAME}} vary depending on which of the {{WITHDRAWAL_METHODS}} is selected, so it is worth checking the specific limits attached to a preferred method before requesting a payout. Most methods carry a minimum withdrawal amount, generally a modest figure designed to keep transaction costs reasonable relative to the payout size, along with a maximum amount that may apply per transaction, per day, or per month depending on the platform's policy and the payment provider's own rules.

Processing time is a separate factor from the limit itself, though the two are often discussed together. Some methods process within a few hours once approved internally, while others, particularly bank-based transfers, can take several business days to complete on the provider's end after leaving the platform. The internal review period, during which the platform confirms the request and checks that identity verification is complete, is generally the same regardless of method, but the time the funds then take to actually arrive depends heavily on which option was chosen.

Checking the cashier section for the current limits and estimated processing time before submitting a withdrawal avoids any surprise, particularly for players hoping to withdraw a larger amount that might need to be split across more than one request.
