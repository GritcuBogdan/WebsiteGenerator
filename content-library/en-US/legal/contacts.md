### Live Chat

Live chat is the fastest route to the support team and is available continuously. It is best suited to time-sensitive questions — for example confirming the status of a pending cash-out, resolving a sign-in issue, or clarifying the terms of an active promotion. Agents are trained across account management, payments, promotion terms and responsible-play tools, and can escalate more complex matters internally when required.

### Email

For queries that require attaching documents — such as identity-verification files or a detailed description of a dispute — email is generally the more suitable channel. Please include the registered account email address and, where relevant, any transaction reference numbers so that the team can locate the account and history quickly.

### Information Useful to Have Ready

To help the support team resolve a query as quickly as possible it is helpful to have the following ready before starting a conversation:

- The email address linked to the account.
- Any relevant transaction, promotion or reference numbers.
- A clear, specific description of the issue, including approximate dates and times where relevant.
- Screenshots of any error messages, if applicable.

### Formal Complaints Procedure

If a matter raised through live chat or email cannot be resolved to your satisfaction at the first point of contact, you have the right to escalate it through the formal complaints procedure.

Initial contact — Raise the issue through live chat or email, clearly stating that you wish to file a formal complaint if the matter is not resolved satisfactorily at this stage.

Internal review — The complaint is logged and reviewed by a senior member of the support team, typically within five business days.

Written response — You receive a written response outlining the outcome of the review and the reasoning behind it.

Escalation if unresolved — If you remain unsatisfied with the outcome you may escalate the matter to an independent alternative dispute-resolution body recognised under the Government of Curacao licence, or to the relevant Dutch regulatory authority, as described in the Terms and Conditions.

Every complaint is handled fairly, transparently and within a reasonable timeframe, and records of all formal complaints and their outcomes are retained in line with regulatory obligations.

### Reporting a Technical Issue

If you encounter a technical issue — such as a title failing to load, an incorrect balance display or a site error — please report it via live chat as soon as possible, including the specific title name, the approximate time the issue occurred and, if possible, a screenshot. Technical issues affecting wager settlement are handled in accordance with the Betting Rules.

### Responsible-Play Enquiries

If you wish to activate a responsible-play tool, discuss concerns about your own play, or ask about self-exclusion, you can do so confidentially through live chat or email. Support staff are trained to handle these conversations sensitively and without judgement, and can guide you through the available options described in full on the Responsible Gaming page.
