### Cookies Policy

This Cookies Policy explains how {{BRAND_NAME}} employs cookies and comparable tracking technologies when you visit the website, and how you can control or adjust these settings. It should be read together with the Privacy Policy, which addresses the broader handling of personal information.

### What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They enable the site to recognise your device on later visits, remember preferences, and gather information about how the site is used. Cookies do not usually contain personally identifiable information on their own, although they can be linked to an account once you sign in.

### Categories of Cookies We Employ

| Category | Purpose | Can Be Disabled? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strictly Necessary | Enable core functions such as sign-in, security and cashier access | No — required for the site to operate |
| Performance | Collect anonymised data on visitor behaviour to help improve the site | Yes |
| Functionality | Remember choices such as language or display preferences | Yes |
| Targeting / Advertising | Track browsing patterns to show more relevant promotional content | Yes |

### Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function correctly. Without them, core features such as signing in, maintaining a session while moving between pages, and completing a deposit or cash-out would not work properly. Because they are required for basic operation they cannot be disabled through our cookie settings tool, although you can block all cookies via your browser settings at the cost of significant site functionality.

### Performance Cookies

Performance cookies gather anonymised information about how visitors interact with the site — for example which pages are visited most often, how long visitors typically remain on a page, and whether errors occur during use. This data helps identify and resolve usability issues and improve the site over time. No personally identifiable information is collected through these cookies.

### Functionality Cookies

These cookies remember choices made while using the site, such as preferred language, display currency, or whether a particular notification has already been dismissed. Disabling them means the site may not retain preferences between visits, requiring them to be reset each time.

### Targeting and Advertising Cookies

These cookies monitor browsing activity on our site and, in some cases, other websites in order to build a profile of interests and display promotional content that is more relevant. They may also measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns. These cookies are optional and can be disabled without affecting core site features.

### Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on the site are placed by third-party services used for analytics, payment processing or marketing. These third parties maintain their own privacy and cookie policies governing how they use the data collected; reviewing those policies is recommended if more detail on a specific third-party cookie is required.

| Third-Party Category | Example Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Analytics providers | Measuring site traffic and usage patterns |
| Payment processors | Enabling secure deposit and cash-out functions |
| Marketing platforms | Measuring the performance of promotional campaigns |

### Managing Cookie Preferences

On the first visit a cookie-consent banner appears, allowing you to accept all cookies, reject all non-essential cookies, or customise preferences by category. Preferences can be revisited and changed at any time through the cookie-settings link normally found in the site footer.

In addition to the on-site tool, most web browsers let you control cookies through their own settings menus, including blocking all cookies, blocking third-party cookies specifically, or deleting cookies already stored on the device. Instructions vary by browser and are usually available in the browser’s help documentation.

| Browser | Typical Location of Cookie Settings |
| --- | --- |
| Google Chrome | Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data |
| Mozilla Firefox | Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data |
| Safari | Preferences > Privacy |
| Microsoft Edge | Settings > Cookies and site permissions |

Disabling strictly necessary cookies through the browser rather than the on-site tool may prevent parts of the site from functioning correctly, including the ability to sign in or complete transactions.

### Retention Periods

Different cookies remain on a device for different lengths of time. Session cookies are deleted automatically when the browser is closed, while persistent cookies remain for a set period — ranging from a few days to up to two years depending on their purpose — unless deleted manually beforehand.

### Do-Not-Track Signals

Some browsers offer a “Do Not Track” signal that can be enabled in settings. Because no unified industry standard currently exists for how websites should respond to this signal, the site does not alter its behaviour in response to Do-Not-Track requests, although individual cookie categories can still be managed through the on-site settings tool.

### Updates to This Policy

This Cookies Policy may be revised periodically to reflect changes in the cookies and technologies used or in applicable law. Reviewing this page from time to time is encouraged in order to stay informed.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Will disabling cookies stop me from using the site completely?

Disabling strictly necessary cookies will prevent core functions such as signing in or making a deposit. Disabling performance, functionality or targeting cookies will not prevent use of the site, although some personalisation features may not behave as expected.

#### Do you use cookies to track gambling activity for advertising purposes?

Targeting cookies may be used to display promotional content based on general browsing behaviour, but this is separate from the responsible-play monitoring described in the Privacy Policy, which relies on account-level data rather than cookies.

#### Can cookie preferences be changed after the first visit?

Yes. Preferences can be updated at any time through the cookie-settings link in the site footer.

#### Do cookies expire automatically?

Yes. Most cookies have a set expiry period after which they are automatically removed from the device, although they can also be deleted manually at any time through browser settings.
