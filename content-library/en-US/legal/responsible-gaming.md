### Responsible Gaming at {{BRAND_NAME}}

{{BRAND_NAME}} treats gambling as entertainment that should never become a source of financial strain or personal difficulty. Most members enjoy machines, table games and live tables purely for leisure, yet a minority may find their habits harder to manage over time. This page outlines the practical controls you can activate to keep play in check, the early indicators that may signal a developing problem, and the independent organisations that can offer further help if needed.

### Our Approach

Operating under a licence from the Government of Curacao, {{BRAND_NAME}} is required to supply effective responsible-play tools rather than treating them as optional extras. Every account receives free access to the full suite of controls described below. No explanation is needed to activate any tool; you may impose a limit or exclude yourself simply because you decide to do so, at any moment.

Promotional messaging is never directed at members who have already activated self-exclusion or a cooling-off period. Support staff receive training to recognise language that may point to distress and to supply factual information rather than encouragement to continue playing.

### Deposit Caps

Deposit caps let you restrict the total sum that can be added to your balance within a daily, weekly or monthly window. Once activated, a new lower limit takes effect at once, while any increase to an existing limit is normally delayed by 24 hours to allow time for reconsideration.

| Cap Type | Function | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily Deposit Cap | Restricts total deposits inside a rolling 24-hour window | Account Settings > Responsible Gaming |
| Weekly Deposit Cap | Restricts total deposits inside a 7-day window | Account Settings > Responsible Gaming |
| Monthly Deposit Cap | Restricts total deposits inside a calendar month | Account Settings > Responsible Gaming |

### Loss Caps

A loss cap limits net losses (deposits minus winnings) over a chosen period rather than the raw amount deposited. This offers a more accurate way to control the overall cost of play because it takes both spending and returns into account.

### Session Length Controls and Reality Checks

Long unbroken sessions can make it easy to lose track of time. Session-length controls automatically end your login after a period you select, while reality checks display a pop-up at intervals you choose (for example every 30, 60 or 120 minutes) showing session duration, net result and total time spent logged in. Neither tool requires justification, and both can be adjusted at any time from account settings, subject to the usual cooling-off delay when moving to a stricter setting.

### Cooling-Off Periods

A cooling-off period suspends access for a duration you select, typically from 24 hours up to several weeks. During the period you cannot log in and promotional messages are paused. Unlike self-exclusion, the suspension ends automatically when the chosen time expires and normal access resumes.

### Self-Exclusion

Self-exclusion is the strongest control available and is intended for situations in which a member wishes to stop using the platform completely for a substantial period. Once activated it blocks all login attempts, cancels active promotions and stops all marketing messages for the selected duration.

| Option | Length | Early Reversal Possible? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short-term | 6 months | No |
| Medium-term | 1 year | No |
| Long-term | 5 years | No |
| Permanent | Indefinite | No |

Self-exclusion cannot be shortened or cancelled before the chosen period ends, even at the member’s own request, because the purpose of the tool is to remove the possibility of impulsive reversal during a moment of vulnerability. If you are uncertain about the appropriate length, selecting a longer period is generally safer; a shorter period that proves insufficient simply allows the cycle to continue.

### Recognising Possible Problem Play

Problematic habits often develop gradually, and many people fail to notice the early indicators in themselves. Common warning signs include:

- Regularly spending more time or money than originally planned.
- Feeling restless, irritable or anxious when attempting to reduce or stop.
- Increasing stake sizes or deposit amounts in an effort to recover previous losses.
- Using gambling to escape stress, anxiety or other difficult feelings rather than for leisure.
- Concealing the amount of time or money spent from family or friends.
- Neglecting work, relationships or other responsibilities because of time spent playing.
- Borrowing money, using credit facilities or selling possessions to fund further play.
- If several of these apply to you or someone close to you, activating one of the tools above and speaking with a trained professional is advisable.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Can a self-exclusion period be ended early if I change my mind?

No. Self-exclusion runs for its full selected length and cannot be shortened or reversed early, even at the member’s request; that permanence is the protection the tool is designed to supply.

#### Does activating a deposit cap involve any charge?

No. All responsible-play tools are free and available to every account without restriction.

#### Will marketing messages continue during a cooling-off period?

No. Promotional communications are paused automatically for the duration of any active cooling-off period or self-exclusion.

#### Can a family member activate self-exclusion on my behalf?

Generally no — self-exclusion must be activated by the account holder for security reasons, although a concerned relative may contact support to discuss available options.
