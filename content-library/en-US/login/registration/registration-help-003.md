The email address is not just a login credential. It is also where confirmation links, account notices, and password recovery messages are sent, so using an address you check regularly matters beyond the moment of signup. Personal details like name, date of birth, and address are collected upfront because they are checked again later during identity verification, which is why accuracy here saves time down the line rather than just satisfying the form.

Currency selection, where offered, determines how the account balance and transactions are displayed, and in most cases cannot be changed easily after the account is created, so choosing carefully at signup is worth the extra thought. Marketing preferences, usually presented as an optional checkbox, control promotional emails and are separate from account or transaction notices, which continue regardless of that choice.
