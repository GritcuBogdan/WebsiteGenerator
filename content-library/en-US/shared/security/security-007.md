A few habits go a long way toward keeping your account at {{BRAND_NAME}} — and your accounts everywhere else — under your control.

Good habits to keep:
- Use a password you don't reuse on other sites
- Keep the email address on your account secure too
- Set a deposit limit before you start playing
- Log out on any device you don't fully control

None of this replaces your own judgment, but it's a reasonable baseline to start from.
