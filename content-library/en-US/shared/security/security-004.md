Responsible play tools exist because everyone has an off night. Deposit limits,
loss limits, time-outs, and self-exclusion are standard in account settings, and
using them is not an admission of anything — it is the same idea as setting a
budget for any other hobby.

If gambling has stopped feeling like a choice, or has stopped being fun, free and
confidential support services are available in most countries and are worth
contacting sooner rather than later.
