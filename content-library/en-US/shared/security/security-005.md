A handful of habits keep an account in good order. Use a password that is unique
to the site. Keep the email address on the account current, so you actually see
security notices when they arrive. Look over your transaction history now and
then, so nothing on it comes as a surprise weeks later. And if something about
your {{BRAND_NAME}} account looks wrong, raise it rather than waiting to see
whether it sorts itself out.
