question: How can I keep my account secure
---
Use a password you have not used on any other site, keep the email address on the
account up to date so security notices actually reach you, and sign out when you
are on a shared device. If {{BRAND_NAME}} offers an extra verification step at
sign-in, turning it on adds a second layer in case your password is ever exposed
somewhere else.
