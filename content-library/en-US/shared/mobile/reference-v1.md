The mobile experience at {{BRAND_NAME}} is designed for browser use on a phone or tablet. The same account should provide access to the lobby, balance, cashier, settings, support, and responsible play tools. A responsive layout is most useful when it keeps the order of actions clear and does not hide important terms behind a difficult menu.

| Mobile area | What a player should be able to do |
| --- | --- |
| Lobby | Search, filter, and open game information |
| Account | Review profile, limits, and recent activity |
| Cashier | Select a payment route and see its status |
| Support | Open live help or find the contact channel |
| Security | Sign out and review unfamiliar access notices |

Test the page on the device you actually use. Check text size, orientation, loading time, and the placement of the spin or payment controls. Use a private connection for account actions and keep the same responsible play limits on mobile that you use elsewhere.
