A casino is really a stack of small features: a lobby you can search, a cashier
that accepts the methods you already use, an account page that keeps your details
straight, and a layout that behaves on whatever device happens to be in your
hand. The sections that follow look at {{BRAND_NAME}} through each of those,
because that is where the differences between one site and the next actually show
up.
