The welcome offer at {{BRAND_NAME}} should be read as a package with several moving parts, not as a single headline number. New players should compare the maximum bonus, the qualifying deposit, the wagering rule, the expiry period, and the games that contribute. A smaller offer with simple terms can be more useful than a larger offer that leaves little time or choice.

| Offer detail | Practical question |
| --- | --- |
| Bonus value | What amount is credited after a qualifying deposit? |
| Minimum deposit | What is the smallest payment that activates the offer? |
| Contribution | Which games help clear the wagering condition? |
| Time limit | How long does the balance remain active? |
| Cash out | What must happen before winnings can leave the account? |

Review the promotion before depositing and keep a note of the current terms. If the offer requires an opt in, complete it at the correct stage. When the conditions no longer suit your budget, decline the promotion rather than depositing more to chase its maximum value.
