Free spins at {{BRAND_NAME}} give a player a defined number of slot rounds without a separate stake for each spin. The important detail is not only the number of spins. Check the featured title, the time window, the way winnings become bonus credit, and the wagering rule applied to the resulting balance.

| Spin detail | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Number of spins | The exact quantity credited to the account |
| Featured title | Which slot accepts the spins |
| Expiry | When unused spins disappear |
| Winnings | Whether results become cash or bonus balance |
| Turnover | The requirement applied to the resulting amount |

Use the spins on the stated title and avoid switching to another game until you understand the campaign rule. If the spins are credited automatically, check the promotions panel before starting. A fixed number of rounds is a useful way to sample a title, but it is not a guarantee of a win or a reason to continue spending after the campaign ends.
