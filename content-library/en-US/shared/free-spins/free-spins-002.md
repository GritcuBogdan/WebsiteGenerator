Free spins offered at {{BRAND_NAME}} give players a set number of chances to spin a slot without placing a separate stake for each round, making them one of the more approachable promotion types available. While the headline number of spins is what usually catches attention, the details that actually determine the offer's value are the featured title the spins apply to, the time window in which they must be used, and the rule governing how any resulting winnings convert into withdrawable funds.

In most cases, winnings generated from free spins are added as bonus credit rather than immediately withdrawable cash, which means a wagering requirement typically needs to be met before that balance can be cashed out. The specific multiplier attached to this requirement, along with which games count toward meeting it, is published in the promotion terms and is worth checking before using the spins rather than after.

Because spins are usually tied to a specific title chosen by the platform, players should not expect to freely choose which game to use them on. Checking the promotions panel first confirms exactly where the spins apply, avoiding any wasted expectation.
