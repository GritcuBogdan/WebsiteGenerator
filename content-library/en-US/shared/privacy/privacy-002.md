Understanding how {{BRAND_NAME}} handles personal data helps set expectations before an account is even created. Information collected typically falls into a few categories, each tied to a specific purpose rather than gathered indiscriminately. Registration details, such as name and contact information, support basic account operation and communication. Identity and address documents submitted during verification exist specifically to confirm eligibility and meet regulatory requirements. Payment information supports processing deposits and withdrawals and plays a role in fraud prevention, while technical data collected through normal platform use helps maintain security and service reliability.

Retention periods generally follow applicable regulatory requirements rather than an arbitrary internal policy, meaning certain records, particularly those related to verification and transactions, may be kept for a defined period even after an account becomes inactive. Players seeking to review, correct, or request deletion of their personal data should use the contact method specified in the current privacy notice rather than a general support channel, since privacy requests are often handled by a dedicated team.

Reading the full privacy notice before registering, rather than after a question arises, is the most reliable way to understand exactly what applies to a given account.
