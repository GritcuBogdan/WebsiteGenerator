Recovering access to an account at {{BRAND_NAME}} after forgetting a password follows a deliberately simple process, designed to get a player back in without compromising security in the process.

Recovery process, step by step:
- Open the sign in page and choose the password recovery option
- Enter the email address registered to the account
- Check the inbox, including spam or promotions folders, for the recovery message
- Confirm the sender address and branding look correct before opening the link
- Set a new, unique password and confirm it meets any minimum complexity requirement
- Log in with the new password and review recent account activity

If the recovery email does not arrive within several minutes, the most likely explanation is a typo in the entered email address rather than a delivery delay, so it is worth trying the request again with careful attention to spelling. A recovery link that has already been used, or has expired, will simply prompt for a new request rather than granting access, so requesting a fresh link is the correct response in that situation rather than repeating the same one.
